MariaDB connection and sync recovery

QA continuation for the 12 October 2026 audit. Scope: shared connection handling, sync startup recovery, and connected-till presence.

The normal rebuilt app lists both connected tills correctly, and Refresh / Sync Now work. Native checks also exercised delayed connection, outage recovery, and the manual retry's failure path. The limits of each observation are recorded below; successful manual retry and every internal gate were not separately traced. Testing and recoverable QA cleanup are complete.

What changed

  • Both connection entry points now share one pool lifecycle and a practical 10-second cold-connection deadline.
  • Late native loads and closes are serialized; resets, credential/URI changes, and stale callbacks cannot close or publish a newer pool.
  • Sync startup has coordinated retry/recovery rather than a permanently retained transient timeout. Readiness still requires identity, schema, and financial recovery to complete.
  • Connected-till queries publish/read presence only when startup is ready and their captured mode/configuration is still current.

Isolation and limitations

  • The existing MariaDB service on port 3306 was not interrupted. Native failure tests use a new private loopback server on port 65160 and a no-traffic-logging delay proxy on port 65161.
  • The original QA profile and WebKit data were recoverably preserved. After testing, the disposable profile/WebKit data were moved to recoverable evidence directories and the original QA profile/WebKit data restored. Read-only SQLite checks confirmed local-only mode and the saved checkout draft remained present. Nothing was deleted.
  • No Windows installer or Windows-device retest has been performed. The Mac checks do not certify Windows deployment or physical two-laptop reconnection.
  • N1 and N6 share one normal-app relaunch. N3 and N4 are a single private-server interruption/recovery sequence, with server evidence only; the UI readiness transitions and individual startup gates were not traced.
  • N5 verifies native retry failure handling only. Successful manual startup retry and internal startup non-overlap were not separately proved natively; the latter is covered by automated tests. The later cold-start recovery was automatic, not a successful manual retry.
  • Cleanup completed: QA app exited, private MariaDB and delay proxy stopped, and original QA profile restored. The existing port-3306 MariaDB service and latest normal app remain running; the normal app was left on Sync Dashboard with two connected tills. Database connection settings, firewall, and credentials were not changed.
