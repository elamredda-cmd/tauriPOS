What changed

  • Ordinary trolley drafts now save on this device in SQLite, with a synchronous WebView mirror covering an imminent reload. Draft recovery does not require MariaDB and is not synchronized to another till.
  • Revisioned clear tombstones and atomic compare-and-swap stop an older pending save from reviving a cleared trolley, including a command arriving from an older WebView.
  • Cart lines, quantities, prices, notes, manual flags, selected line, customer and discount references are retained. Payment tender/method/modal state, staff credentials and customer account balances are not saved in the draft.
  • Checkout startup checks saved sale/hold ownership before recovery; malformed copies are kept for investigation rather than silently overwritten. Write failures remain retryable.
  • Three startup NULL-normalization migrations avoid re-updating normalized rows while retaining legacy repairs, preventing needless catalogue/FTS update cascades.

Safety and limits

QA uses com.elamreda.tauri-app.native-qa and its isolated mock-data profile. The original app/trolley and live business data are not modified for these checks; provider keys and registrations are not test targets. Only isolated mock cash/hold records were used. No live provider requests were made; physical printer/drawer actions are not certified. Manual discount round-trip was automated-only because no active discount was available in this mock profile.

Windows/WebView2, physical terminals and other hardware still require separate testing. This report's normal build is macOS arm64 debug, not a release or Windows certification. The forced-restart case needed one clean relaunch after a blank renderer, as recorded above.

The isolated QA app was left open with an empty ordinary trolley and a durable null draft. One 169p mock held trolley and three completed 169p mock cash receipts remain as test evidence; there are zero pending online payment intents.
