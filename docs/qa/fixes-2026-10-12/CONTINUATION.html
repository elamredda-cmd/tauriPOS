AUDIT-03 P2

Failed settings saves can still appear saved

/settings/feedback · /settings/scale · /settings/printers · /settings/advanced

implementation completeNative observation
Fix

Feedback, scale, printer and tax-mode settings publish only after saveSettingsBatch succeeds. Audited before-values and sanitized audit rows are prepared before commit; settings, audit records and sync outbox mutations then share the atomic local batch, avoiding a post-commit audit-read/write failure that could falsely report an already saved setting as failed.
